The Right to Be Wrong

Everything in this notebook — Scrolls, Cantrips, Distillations, the occasional Transmutation — is a snapshot of what I believed at the moment I hit publish, not a certified spellbook. I reserve the right to have been wrong (I already have been, more than once — ask me about the "definitely final" architecture decision from 2021). Treat every post as a lab notebook entry from a working alchemist, not a peer-reviewed grimoire.

A Living Document, Not a Monument

Posts here get revised. When I learn something that contradicts an earlier scroll, I'd rather update the potion than pretend the old formula still works. If a post changes materially after publication, I'll say so in the post itself rather than quietly editing history — but I won't promise a changelog for every typo fix or rewritten paragraph. The Notebook is a workshop, not a museum.

On the Transmutations (and the Other AI Familiars)

Some posts are marked Transmutation — research-dump / AI-assisted deep-dives, conducted with an AI familiar (Claude, mostly) rather than handcrafted line by line the way the older Scrolls were. That's a deliberate, branded stream, not a secret. The /now and /uses pages talk about how that conducting actually works day to day. Distillations are condensed link-plus-commentary posts, and both carry an assay label — my own read on how battle-tested the claims inside actually are, from speculative to proven — right alongside the post-type badge. None of that changes the first two rules above: AI-assisted or handcrafted, it's all still subject to being wrong and subject to revision.

Disagree With Me? Good.

If something here is wrong, incomplete, or has aged badly, I'd rather hear about it than have it sit uncorrected. Send a letter — magical correspondence is what that page is for. I can't promise I'll agree, but I can promise I'll read it, and that being told I'm wrong in public is a risk I've already accepted by publishing in the open.

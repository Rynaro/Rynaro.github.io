Character Sheet

Character Bio

Origin: {{ site.data.character_build.character.origin }}

Era I — The Apprentice. I cut my teeth on Object Pascal (Delphi, if you want the receipts — it's how half of Brazil learned to code) before Ruby found me, and the lineage from one to the other still shapes how I think about syntax today. I even learned English this way: one dialogue box at a time, grinding through Final Fantasy VII — still the best tutor I've ever had (sorry, actual teachers).

Era II — The Artificer. Once syntax stopped being the interesting part, I got obsessed with shape: Clean Architecture, Domain-Driven Design, use-cases — the kind of ideas that make you rewrite a perfectly working feature three times because you finally understand why it should look different. That obsession eventually distilled into Fluxus, a small, dependency-less use-case wrapper for Ruby, built because I couldn't find one that stayed out of my way.

Era III — The Guildmaster. Somewhere along the way people started asking me to lead instead of just build — and, to my own surprise, I liked it. At ElectionBuddy that grew into Director of Engineering; alongside it I built Diurnata, a small tool guarding the one resource every team actually runs on — human focus and well-being. Leading, it turns out, is its own architecture problem.

Era IV — The Technomancer. These days the tools have changed shape again. I spend as much time conducting AI familiars — routing between Claude, GPT, and whichever local model is awake that day — as I do writing Ruby by hand. Less "the machines replaced me," more "I finally have a party." Symbiotic Technomancer stopped being just a trait on this page; it's closer to a job description now.

When I'm not casting code or leading teams, you'll find me tending my garden of ideas: game design experiments, smart organizational tools, and even literal plants — from seed to bloom.

Ever curious. Ever building. Ever refining.

The journey of a Code Alchemist never ends.
